The **Carciofo Spinoso di Sardegna** is a PDO product and is recognisable by its leaves with visible spines. Cleaning it takes a little patience, but the result pays off.

## How to clean it

1. Remove the tough outer leaves, down to the lighter and tender ones.
2. Cut off the top with the spines.
3. Peel the stem, which is edible.
4. Keep the artichokes in water with lemon so they do not darken.

## How to eat it

- **Raw**, in thin slices with oil and lemon, when tender.
- **Fried**, cut into wedges.
- **Stuffed**, with breadcrumbs and herbs.
- **Baked or pan-cooked**, with garlic and parsley.

Wear gloves if the spines bother you. Tenderness depends on the season and the plant.
