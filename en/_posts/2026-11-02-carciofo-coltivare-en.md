The artichoke is a **perennial** plant: once planted it produces for several years.

## Seeds or offshoots?

- **Offshoots**: these are the shoots that grow at the base of the plant. They are used to propagate it and it is the most common method, because the new plant stays identical to the mother.
- **Seeds**: you can sow them, but the plants may be less uniform.

## Basic care

- Well-drained soil and a sunny spot.
- Regular watering, without waterlogging.
- Space between plants: they are large, so do not plant them too close.
- Protection from hard frost in colder areas.

Sowing and transplanting times depend on the climate and on the variety: follow the instructions on the packet.
